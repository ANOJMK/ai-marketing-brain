OAuth 2.0 Compliance

1

User Authorization

Users are redirected to the official authorization page of the selected platform before any access is granted.

2

Permission Grant

Only the permissions explicitly approved by the user are requested and used by the application.

3

Access Token

OAuth access tokens are securely stored and protected using industry-standard security practices.

4

Revocation

Users may revoke application access at any time from the connected platform or by contacting Brain Africa Labs.

Privacy & Data Protection

Data Minimization

Only the information required to provide requested services is processed.

No Password Collection

AI Marketing Brain never requests or stores passwords for supported social media accounts.

Secure Processing

Personal information is processed using secure infrastructure and encrypted communications.

User Control

Users remain in full control of their connected accounts and permissions.

Data Retention

Brain Africa Labs retains personal information only for the period necessary to provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain platform security.

Users may request deletion of their information through our Data Deletion page.

User Rights

Access

Deletion

Consent Withdrawal

Request Data Deletion

AI Marketing Brain respects your privacy. You may request the deletion of your account information and authorization tokens at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information associated with AI Marketing Brain.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to our support team using the email address associated with your account.

support@aimarketingbrain.com

After Your Request

  • Your identity may be verified.
  • OAuth authorization tokens will be revoked.
  • Stored personal information will be removed unless legal obligations require retention.

Processing Time

Most deletion requests are completed within 30 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the process has been completed.

Privacy Commitment

AI Marketing Brain only accesses social media accounts through official OAuth 2.0 authorization. We never request or store user passwords. Users may revoke access or request permanent deletion of their data at any time.

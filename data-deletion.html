Request Data Deletion

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your personal information and associated authorization tokens.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to:

support@aimarketingbrain.com

Include the email address associated with your account.

What Happens Next?

  • Your identity may be verified.
  • Authorization tokens will be revoked where applicable.
  • Your stored personal data will be deleted unless retention is required by law.

Processing Time

Requests are generally processed within 30 days.

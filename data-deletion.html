Privacy

Request Data Deletion

Brain Africa Labs respects your privacy. Users may request the deletion of their personal information and authorization data associated with AI Marketing Brain at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information stored by Brain Africa Labs in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account information, send an email using the email address associated with your account.

privacy@brainafricalabs.com

What Happens Next

  • Your identity may be verified before processing.
  • OAuth authorization tokens will be revoked.
  • Associated personal information will be permanently deleted unless retention is required by law.
  • You will no longer be able to use connected services until you authorize them again.

Processing Time

Most deletion requests are completed within 30 business days. A confirmation email will be sent once the deletion process has been completed.

Privacy Commitment

AI Marketing Brain connects to social media platforms using official OAuth 2.0 authorization. Brain Africa Labs never requests or stores users' social media passwords. Users may revoke access or request permanent deletion of their information at any time.

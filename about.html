About AI Marketing Brain

AI Marketing Brain is an AI-powered marketing automation platform designed to help businesses, creators and marketing teams create, review, schedule and publish content across multiple social media platforms using official APIs..

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify digital marketing by combining Artificial Intelligence with secure official platform integrations, allowing users to produce high-quality content faster while maintaining full control over publication.

Key Features

  • AI Content Generation
  • Marketing Copy Assistant
  • Campaign Planning
  • Content Review Workflow
  • Official API Publishing
  • Performance Analytics
  • Secure OAuth 2.0 Authentication

Supported Platforms

  • TikTok
  • Facebook
  • Instagram
  • LinkedIn
  • YouTube
  • X (formerly Twitter)

Security & Privacy

AI Marketing Brain uses OAuth 2.0 authentication for supported platforms. Passwords are never collected User authorization is always required before any connected social media account can be accessed.

Technology

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Official Social Media APIs
  • OAuth 2.0 Authentication
  • HTTPS Secure Communication
  • Cloud Automation Workflows

Our Vision

We aim to build a secure and intelligent marketing platform that helps businesses automate repetitive tasks while respecting user privacy, platform policies and official API requirements.

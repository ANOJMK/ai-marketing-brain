How AI Marketing Brain Works

AI Marketing Brain helps users create, review, schedule and publish content across supported social media platforms using official APIs and secure OAuth 2.0 authentication.

Step 1 — Connect Your Account

The user connects their TikTok account using the official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. AI Marketing Brain never asks for or stores the user's TikTok password.

Step 2 — Generate AI Content

The platform assists the user in creating captions, hashtags, marketing copy and content ideas using artificial intelligence.

Step 3 — Review Before Publishing

Every piece of content is reviewed by the user before publication. AI Marketing Brain does not automatically publish content without explicit user approval.

Step 4 — Publish via Official APIs

Once approved by the user, the content is published through the official TikTok Content Posting API or other supported platform APIs, depending on the connected account.

Step 5 — Analytics & Performance

After publication, users can review available analytics and campaign performance to improve future marketing strategies.

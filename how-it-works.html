How AI Marketing Brain Works

AI Marketing Brain uses Artificial Intelligence together with official social media APIs to help users create, review and publish marketing content securely.

1

Connect Your Social Account

Users securely connect their TikTok or supported social media account through the official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. AI Marketing Brain never asks for social media passwords.

2

Create AI Content

Artificial Intelligence generates captions, hashtags, titles, marketing copy and publishing suggestions tailored to the selected platform.

3

Review Before Publishing

Every generated post is reviewed and approved by the user before any publishing request is sent.

4

Publish Using Official APIs

After approval, AI Marketing Brain submits the content through official APIs such as the TikTok Content Posting API.

5

Monitor Performance

Users can analyze campaign performance and improve future content strategies using analytics dashboards.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

About AI Marketing Brain

AI Marketing Brain is an AI-powered marketing platform that helps users create, review, schedule and publish content across supported social media platforms using official APIs.

Information We Collect

  • Name (when provided)
  • Email address
  • OAuth authorization information
  • Marketing content created by users
  • Technical logs required to operate the platform

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide AI Marketing Brain services.
  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Enable publishing through official APIs.
  • Improve platform performance and security.

OAuth Authentication

AI Marketing Brain uses official OAuth 2.0 authentication. We never request or store your passwords for TikTok or any other social media platform.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only when required to provide the requested service or when required by applicable law.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

  • HTTPS encrypted communication
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • Secure storage of authorization tokens
  • Restricted access to sensitive information

Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our support team.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

About AI Marketing Brain

AI Marketing Brain is an AI-powered marketing platform that helps businesses, creators and agencies generate, review, schedule and publish marketing content using official social media APIs.

Information We Collect

AI Marketing Brain collects only the information necessary to provide, maintain and improve its services. The type of information collected depends on the features you use and the permissions you explicitly grant.

Information You Provide

  • Full name (when voluntarily provided)
  • Email address
  • Company or organization name
  • Support requests and communications
  • Marketing content created within the platform
  • Campaign settings and publishing preferences

Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Language preferences
  • Time zone
  • Usage statistics
  • Error logs
  • Security logs

Technical information is collected solely to improve reliability, performance and security.

Information Received Through Connected Platforms

When you connect your TikTok or other supported social media account, AI Marketing Brain receives only the information explicitly authorized by you through the official OAuth 2.0 authorization process.

Depending on the permissions granted, the platform may receive:

  • Public profile information
  • Platform user identifier
  • Display name
  • Profile picture URL
  • Authorization tokens
  • Publishing permissions
  • Basic account metadata

AI Marketing Brain does not receive any information that has not been explicitly authorized by the user.

OAuth Permissions Requested

AI Marketing Brain requests only the minimum permissions required to deliver the services requested by users.

Depending on the connected platform, requested permissions may include:

  • User authentication
  • Basic profile identification
  • Content publishing
  • Media upload
  • Publishing status
  • Analytics access (when authorized)

Each permission is displayed during the official OAuth authorization screen. Users remain free to approve or decline these permissions.

Permissions may also be revoked at any time directly from the connected platform.

How We Use Your Information

AI Marketing Brain uses collected information exclusively for legitimate business purposes.

  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Provide AI-powered marketing services.
  • Generate marketing content.
  • Publish authorized content.
  • Maintain user sessions.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Detect security incidents.
  • Improve system performance.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Your personal information is never used for unrelated commercial purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, AI Marketing Brain processes personal information based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • User consent.
  • Performance of a contract.
  • Compliance with applicable laws.
  • Legitimate business interests.
  • Protection against fraud and abuse.

When consent is required, users may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Information We Never Collect

AI Marketing Brain is designed to minimize data collection.

We never request or store:

  • TikTok passwords
  • Facebook passwords
  • Instagram passwords
  • LinkedIn passwords
  • YouTube passwords
  • X (Twitter) passwords
  • Bank card information
  • Government identification documents unless legally required
  • Private messages from connected social platforms

Authentication is performed exclusively through official OAuth 2.0 authorization.

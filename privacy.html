Privacy Policy

Brain Africa Labs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how AI Marketing Brain collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • OAuth authorization information
  • Basic profile information provided by authorized platforms
  • Application usage information
  • Technical information such as browser type and device information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide requested services
  • Authenticate your account using official OAuth 2.0
  • Improve platform performance
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain platform security

3. OAuth Authentication

AI Marketing Brain connects to supported social platforms using official OAuth authorization. We never request or store your social media passwords.


4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.


5. Data Sharing

Brain Africa Labs does not sell your personal information. Information is only shared when required to provide requested services or when required by applicable law.


6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.


7. Your Rights

  • Request access to your information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke OAuth authorization at any time

1. Introduction

Welcome to AI Marketing Brain. AI Marketing Brain is an Artificial Intelligence powered marketing automation platform designed to help businesses, content creators, marketing professionals, and organizations create, review, schedule, manage, and publish digital content using official social media platform APIs.

Protecting your privacy is one of our highest priorities. We believe users should always understand what information is collected, why it is collected, how it is processed, how long it is retained, and the choices available regarding their personal information.

This Privacy Policy applies whenever you access our website, connect a supported social media account, use our AI-powered tools, or interact with AI Marketing Brain in any other way.

By accessing or using AI Marketing Brain, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The official AI Marketing Brain website.

  • Connected social media accounts.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that operate independently from AI Marketing Brain. When you use TikTok, Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, or other connected platforms, their own Privacy Policies and Terms of Service continue to apply.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following definitions apply.

  • Platform means AI Marketing Brain.

5. OAuth Authentication & Connected Platforms

AI Marketing Brain connects users to supported social media platforms exclusively through the official OAuth 2.0 authorization framework provided by each platform.

During the authorization process, users authenticate directly with the respective platform. AI Marketing Brain never asks users to disclose their social media passwords.

Once authorization is granted, the connected platform may provide limited account information and temporary authorization credentials (access tokens) that allow AI Marketing Brain to perform only the actions explicitly authorized by the user.

Users remain in full control of their connected accounts and may revoke authorization at any time through the settings of the respective social media platform.

6. OAuth Permissions Requested

AI Marketing Brain requests only the minimum permissions required to provide the services selected by the user. We follow the principle of least privilege, meaning that no unnecessary permissions are requested.

Depending on the connected platform and the features used, AI Marketing Brain may request one or more of the following permissions:

  • Authenticate the user through OAuth 2.0.
  • Access basic public profile information.
  • Upload images or videos selected by the user.
  • Create draft posts.
  • Publish content after explicit user confirmation.
  • Retrieve publication status.
  • Access analytics when authorized by the user.

Every permission requested is clearly displayed by the connected platform during the official authorization process. Users remain free to approve or deny any authorization request.

AI Marketing Brain never attempts to bypass platform security, request hidden permissions, or perform unauthorized actions.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

AI Marketing Brain uses cookies and similar technologies to improve security, maintain user sessions, remember preferences, and enhance the overall user experience.

AI Marketing Brain retains personal information only for as long as necessary to provide requested services and comply with legal obligations.

Examples of retention periods

Protecting user information is one of our highest priorities. AI Marketing Brain implements commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

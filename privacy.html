Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

About AI Marketing Brain

AI Marketing Brain is an AI-powered marketing platform that helps businesses, creators and agencies generate, review, schedule and publish marketing content using official social media APIs.

Information We Collect

  • Name (when provided)
  • Email address
  • OAuth authorization information
  • User-generated marketing content
  • Technical logs required for platform operation

How We Use Information

  • Provide AI Marketing Brain services
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Enable publishing through official APIs
  • Improve platform performance
  • Protect against fraud and abuse

OAuth Authentication

AI Marketing Brain only uses official OAuth 2.0 authentication. We never request or store TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or other social media passwords.

Data Sharing

We never sell personal information. Information is shared only when required to provide our services or when legally required.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to provide services, maintain security or comply with legal obligations.

Security

  • HTTPS encrypted communication
  • OAuth 2.0 authorization
  • Secure storage of access tokens
  • Restricted internal access
  • Regular security monitoring

Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information at any time. Users may also revoke OAuth authorization directly from the connected platform.

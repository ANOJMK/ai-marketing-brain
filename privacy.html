Privacy Policy

Brain Africa Labs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how AI Marketing Brain collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • OAuth authorization information
  • Basic profile information provided by authorized platforms
  • Application usage information
  • Technical information such as browser type and device information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide requested services
  • Authenticate your account using official OAuth 2.0
  • Improve platform performance
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain platform security

3. OAuth Authentication

AI Marketing Brain connects to supported social media platforms using official OAuth authorization. We never request or store your passwords.


4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.


5. Data Sharing

Brain Africa Labs does not sell your personal information. Information is only shared when necessary to provide our services or when required by law.


6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.


7. Your Rights

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information through our Data Deletion Request page.
  • Revoke OAuth authorization at any time.

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us:

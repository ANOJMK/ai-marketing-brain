Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. About AI Marketing Brain

AI Marketing Brain is a marketing automation platform that helps users create, review and publish content across supported social media platforms using official APIs.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • OAuth authorization information
  • Content created or uploaded by the user
  • Technical logs required to operate the service

3. Why We Collect Information

  • Provide our services.
  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Allow authorized publishing through official APIs.
  • Improve platform reliability and security.

4. OAuth Authentication

AI Marketing Brain uses official OAuth 2.0 authentication provided by supported social media platforms.

We never request or store user passwords for third-party platforms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information is shared only when necessary to provide the requested services or when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

  • HTTPS encryption
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • Secure storage of authorization tokens
  • Access limited to authorized processes

8. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information by contacting our support team.
